The Magical Common Beech Tree Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy covers the current version of The Magical Common Beech Tree, including the v6.4 local testing build. It will be updated before live purchases or online content delivery are introduced.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

The Magical Common Beech Tree is a children's illustrated story and colouring app provided by Łukasz Gardecki, using the publishing name Luminous Olive Studio, based in Poland. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that provider.

For privacy questions or requests, contact lookgarden86@gmail.com. For personal information we receive through support correspondence, Łukasz Gardecki is the data controller.

This policy explains how information is handled in the app and when a parent, guardian or other user contacts us about it. It distinguishes information stored on your device from information you choose to send to us. Separate services, such as your email provider, app store or a cloud storage service you select, have their own privacy practices.

2. Privacy at a glance

  • You do not need an app account to read or colour.
  • Artwork, artist names or nicknames, reading positions and preferences are stored locally on your device.
  • Ordinary reading, colouring and playback do not send this local information to us.
  • The app does not contain advertising, behavioural tracking, analytics SDKs or an embedded crash-reporting service in the version covered by this policy.
  • We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.
  • Backups are exported only when you choose to save them, to a destination you select.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message.

3. Children and families

The app is designed for children and families, including children approximately six to twelve years old. Children can read, listen and colour without providing personal information to us or creating an online account.

Artist profiles are local labels used to keep artwork and reading positions separate on the device. They are not online accounts. A nickname or a non-identifying label is sufficient; a child's full name is not required.

Parents and guardians should manage support correspondence and any external services. Please do not send a child's full name, address, school, photographs or other identifying information unless genuinely necessary to resolve a specific request. We do not ask children to submit such information as a condition of using the story or colouring features.

If you believe a child has sent personal information to us unintentionally, contact us. We will assess the request and delete unnecessary information, subject to any applicable legal obligation to retain it. We may request proportionate information to verify a parent's authority, without seeking excessive identifying documents.

4. Information stored on your device

The app stores information needed to provide its features, including:

  • Colouring and drawings saved for each artist profile.
  • Artist names or nicknames you enter and the selected profile.
  • Reading and colouring positions.
  • Drawing preferences, such as selected tools, colours and sizes.
  • Display, sound, narration and page-turning preferences.
  • Tutorial-offer or completion indicators and other local first-use preferences, where implemented.
  • Local content-installation or test-unlock status needed for the installed version.

The purpose is to remember your place, preserve artwork, separate artists' work and apply your choices. This information is held in the app's storage on your device. We do not receive a copy simply because you use these features, and cannot remotely view, restore or delete your local artwork.

Installed illustrations and audio files are app content rather than recordings of your family. The app plays prerecorded narration and ambient audio; it does not need to record your voice. Normal reading, colouring and playback do not require access to your contacts, location, microphone or camera.

5. Backups and files you choose

The app allows a parent or guardian to export and import artwork backups through Android's system file picker. Backup files include saved colouring, artist names and remembered page positions. They do not serve as purchase receipts or restore purchase entitlements.

You select where a backup is stored. If you choose a cloud folder or another external provider, that provider may receive and store the file under its own terms and privacy policy. This is a transfer you initiate; the app does not automatically send backups to us or operate a developer-run cloud synchronisation service.

Treat exported backups as potentially personal files. They are not password-protected by the app and may contain names and artwork. Anyone with access to a backup may be able to read its contents. You control sharing and deletion of exported copies.

The app disables its standard Android automatic backup option. User-directed exports, device migration and device-manufacturer services are distinct processes; consult your device and storage-provider settings where relevant.

6. Support messages

The Contact feature opens your email application. Opening it does not itself send a message. If you choose to send an email, we receive your email address, message content and any attachments or other information included in the email.

We use that information to respond, investigate the issue, keep appropriate correspondence records and establish or defend legal claims where necessary. We do not automatically attach artwork or diagnostic logs to your message. Please review attachments and screenshots before sending them, and remove unnecessary personal information.

The support address currently uses Gmail. Our email service provider processes correspondence to deliver, store and protect email. Google describes its practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Your own email provider also processes the messages you send.

Contacting support does not subscribe you to marketing or a newsletter.

7. Purchases and content installation

The current local testing version does not process payments or download the book from the internet. Its clearly labelled development unlock installs bundled content by copying it into the app's local storage. No real purchase occurs through that test mechanism.

This policy does not represent future Google Play Billing or remote content delivery as already implemented. If a public version introduces those services, we will update this section before that version is released to explain the transaction or entitlement information processed, relevant service providers and any network activity.

Separately, Google Play may process information when you obtain, update, review or otherwise interact with the app through the store. Android, Google Play and device services operate under their own settings and privacy notices. The absence of app-embedded analytics does not mean those independent services process no information.

8. App guidance and narration

Story narration, tutorial instructions and other included voice clips are prerecorded content. Playing them locally does not send your voice or artwork to a voice-generation service. Producing the included narration does not require your child to have an ElevenLabs account or submit information to ElevenLabs through the app.

Local preferences may determine whether guidance plays and whether a first-use explanation has already been shown. They are used for the app's operation, not to create an advertising profile or infer a child's emotions, ability or identity.

9. Websites, newsletters and external links

If you choose to open an external website, your browser and the website's providers may process information such as your IP address and request details. This is separate from offline app use. A website visit is governed by the applicable website notice and provider policies.

No newsletter registration or external support-payment service is included in the app covered by this policy. Any future parent newsletter must be optional, have a separate signup notice identifying its provider and purposes, and provide a way to unsubscribe. App purchase or use must not automatically enrol a person in a mailing list.

This privacy page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with visits to this page, including your IP address and request details. This page does not include analytics scripts, advertising, newsletter forms or tracking technologies added by us. Its privacy statement is available at https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Any additional website features, cookies or providers must be covered by an accurate website notice before they are enabled.

10. Disclosure and service providers

We do not upload local artwork, artist labels or reading activity to a developer-operated server. We do not disclose that local information to advertisers or data brokers.

For information you send us directly, access is limited to what is necessary to handle your request and operate the relevant support service. It may be processed by our email provider, or disclosed to professional advisers, authorities or other recipients where legally required or necessary for a legal claim. We do not disclose information for unrelated marketing.

If you independently choose an external storage provider, email service or website, that service may act under its own responsibilities. Its exact role depends on the service and circumstances.

11. Legal grounds for information we receive

Where the GDPR applies to information we receive, we rely on the legal basis appropriate to the purpose:

  • Our legitimate interests in answering ordinary enquiries, resolving technical problems, protecting our services and maintaining proportionate records, balanced against your rights and children's particular privacy interests.
  • Taking steps at your request before a contract, or performing a contract, where necessary for a contractual support or purchase-related request.
  • Compliance with applicable legal obligations where retention or disclosure is required.
  • Consent where a particular optional activity requires it. Any future newsletter consent will be requested separately.

Using the app does not itself constitute consent to marketing, tracking or future data collection. We do not rely on a child's agreement to this policy as permission for online collection.

12. Retention and deletion

Local artwork and preferences remain until removed through available app controls, Android's app-data controls or uninstallation. Clearing all app data removes locally held preferences and artwork. Uninstallation generally removes the app's private data. Exported backups remain wherever you saved them until you delete them separately. Device or cloud-provider copies may be subject to those services' own retention rules.

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve enquiries, provide relevant follow-up support and meet legal obligations. We review retained correspondence and delete information that is no longer needed. We may retain relevant information longer where required by law or reasonably necessary for an unresolved dispute or legal claim, limiting retention to what is needed. Provider backup copies may remain for a limited additional period under the provider's deletion procedures.

You can request deletion of support information by email. We will explain if a legal reason prevents immediate deletion. We cannot delete copies held independently by your email, backup or device provider on your behalf.

13. Security and international processing

We use the app's local storage model to minimise information sent off the device. Protect your device with an appropriate lock and keep it updated. Protect backup files and any cloud account you choose to use.

For information we receive, we use reasonable safeguards appropriate to its nature, including restricting access. No device, email service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Support providers may process information outside Poland or the European Economic Area. Where we are responsible for such transfers, we rely on an applicable adequacy decision or other legally recognised safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, as appropriate to the provider and processing involved. You may contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your correspondence.

14. Your rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and obtain data portability where its legal conditions apply. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

Contact lookgarden86@gmail.com to make a request. We may need proportionate verification before releasing or changing personal information. Where the GDPR applies, we normally respond within one month; permitted extensions will be explained.

These rights concern information we actually hold. For device-only information, the practical controls are in the app, Android settings and your chosen backup destination. There is no developer-held app account to close.

You may complain to your local supervisory authority. In Poland, the authority is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO): https://uodo.gov.pl/.

We do not use the app's local activity for automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you or your child.

15. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when relevant practices change and revise the effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain a separate lawful permission before a new activity begins. Publishing an amended policy is not, by itself, consent to new collection.
